Augur uses a restrained spatial system: clear grouping, square geometry, and enough whitespace to separate hierarchy without turning records interfaces into decorative layouts.

## Frame and measure

- Use a 1200px content frame for wide documentation and application compositions.
- Use 64px outer gutters on wide screens and 24px below the large-layout threshold.
- Keep long-form reading measure near 65 characters where prose is the primary content.
- Use larger composition gaps to separate sections; use the local spacing scale inside components and compact groups.

These values describe composition roles. Representable adopted values remain canonical in `DESIGN.md`; generated token artifacts remain derived outputs.

## Spacing decisions

Prefer spacing that communicates grouping:

- tighter gaps join a label to its control, title to description, or metadata pair;
- medium gaps separate related controls or sections inside a panel;
- large gaps separate page regions and major editorial sections.

Do not add spacing only to make a sparse composition feel more designed. In Augur, whitespace clarifies structure rather than decorating it.

## Control sizing

The standard structural control heights are 32px, 36px, and 40px. Components choose among them according to density and emphasis; touch/coarse-pointer contexts must preserve an actionable target of at least 44×44px where the control is expected to be touched directly.

## Shape language

Augur panels, controls, and Dialog surfaces use square geometry: 0px radius. Intrinsically circular controls such as radio indicators keep their required shape, and supplied identity artwork keeps its own geometry.

Rounded containers, pills, and softened cards are not part of the default surface language. Do not introduce them for decoration or familiarity with another design system.

## Panels and grouping

Use a panel only when it creates a meaningful region. A Card is not the default wrapper for every block. Prefer open page composition when hierarchy can be expressed through spacing, typography, and quiet separators alone.

Panels use clear insets and quiet boundaries. Dense specimens and tables may exceed the prose measure when the content requires it.

## Adaptive behavior

Responsive changes preserve hierarchy and reading order:

- stack columns before compressing content into unreadable widths;
- keep the primary action and primary heading discoverable after reflow;
- keep light and dark themes geometrically identical;
- preserve control usability on coarse pointers;
- allow dense content to span wider than prose when necessary.

Component and pattern pages document only the exceptions or specific application of these shared rules.
