Augur currently uses a live-text identity in system typography because verified production logo masters have not been supplied to this repository. This is an intentional fallback, not an invitation to recreate the missing artwork.

## Current identity

The maintained lockup is two text elements:

| Element | Treatment | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Augur` | Sora 400, sentence case | Primary wordmark voice |
| `Design system` | Schibsted Grotesk 400, uppercase, tracked, secondary color | Quiet descriptor |

The wordmark uses the active theme's foreground color. The descriptor uses the active theme's secondary text role. The lockup does not spend the green accent signal.

In large editorial openings, the wordmark may use the editorial-title role. In compact shell chrome it uses the smaller UI role while preserving the same family, regular weight, and casing.

## Usage rules

- Keep the identity as live text while official masters remain unresolved.
- Keep the wordmark regular weight and sentence case.
- Keep the descriptor visually quieter than the wordmark.
- Let light and dark themes resolve through semantic text roles; do not create fixed-color identity variants.
- Give the lockup clear space appropriate to the surrounding composition rather than placing it in a decorative container.
- Omit the descriptor if the available space cannot support it legibly; do not shrink, condense, or de-track it into a new treatment.

## Do not reconstruct missing artwork

Do not trace, redraw, crop, recolor, approximate, or extract a production mark from the brand PDF. Do not create a substitute SVG, font glyph, favicon mark, or logo component that implies an official master exists.

A neutral interface icon may exist as shell chrome, but it must not drift into a reconstructed brand mark.

## Official artwork replacement rule

When verified official masters are supplied, update the asset provenance record first. The supplied artwork then replaces the text fallback according to the brand foundation's usage rules.

Do not create an intermediate approximation between the current text identity and verified masters.
