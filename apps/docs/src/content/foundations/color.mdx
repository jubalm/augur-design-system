The palette is built around Augur Navy and Augur Green with a
restrained set of paper, graphite, and pewter companions. Navy anchors
dark UI and is the brand anchor; Green is a signal of intent — the
primary action, active state, focus, or a short orienting rule — never
a decorative wash. All fifteen color values are adopted facts recorded
in the `DESIGN.md` front matter; the package generates them into
`--augur-color-*` primitives, and this page paints those generated
values. No hex value is retyped anywhere on it.

## The palette, rendered live

Each chip paints a generated `--augur-color-*` variable from

tokens regenerate, this palette re-renders with the new values.

## Companions and roles

Some values deliberately carry two names in two contexts: **brand
companion names describe colors; surface role names describe jobs.**
The tokens and this page keep the distinction visible:

| Name | Kind | Allowed use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Augur Navy (`primary` token) | Brand companion | Brand anchor, dark canvas, primary text on light surfaces. Never replaced by pure black. |
| Augur Green (`accent` token) | Brand companion | Intent signal on dark surfaces; never decoration. |
| Deep | Brand companion (Green family) | The accessible green on light surfaces: primary actions, focus, signals. |
| Wash | Brand companion (Green family) | Light green fill only; never readable green text. |
| Augur Graphite (`secondary` token) | Brand companion | Secondary text and supporting information on light surfaces. |
| Augur Pewter (`secondary-dark` token) | Brand companion | Secondary text on dark surfaces. |
| Mist | Brand companion (Navy family) | Dark edges, rules, and quiet marks only; never readable copy; not the light quiet region. |
| Raised (role) | Runtime surface role (light) | The elevated light panel job, filled by White. |
| Muted (role) | Runtime surface role (light) | Quiet light regions. |
| Surface 1 / 2 / 3 | Runtime surface roles (dark) | Stepped dark layers for panels, controls, and selected regions. |

## Surfaces and elevation

Depth is tonal, not ornamental. Light surfaces use Paper as the canvas,
White for raised panels, Muted for quiet regions, and Border for
hairlines. Dark surfaces use Navy as the canvas, then Surface 1, Surface
2, and Surface 3 as stepped layers for panels, controls, and selected
regions. There are no decorative shadows, glows, bevels, gradients, or
textures, and the elevation logic is identical in both themes. Border
and Mist are rule colors — never text colors and never replacements for
a surface.

## Semantic roles, not raw values

Components and pages consume the shadcn-compatible semantic role layer
(`--background`, `--card`, `--primary`, `--muted`, `--border`, `--ring`,
and the rest) delivered by the package stylesheet. Roles reference
generated tokens and never raw color values; the full mapping and its
selection model live on [Theming](/foundations/theming). Two mapping
decisions matter most when reading the palette:

- The action slot `--primary` resolves to **Deep on light** and
  **Green on dark** surfaces, so light-theme intent stays within
  contrast rules while dark surfaces carry the brighter brand signal.
  (`--augur-color-primary` remains the Navy companion — the name
  collision is deliberate and documented.)
- `--accent` is a **neutral** hover/highlight surface (Muted in light,
  Surface 2 in dark). Green is a signal, not a resting hover tint, and
  hover never multiplies green marks in a view.

The brand palette defines no danger hue: `--destructive` maps to the
strongest neutral action pairing, and destructive meaning must be
legible in the label.

## Contrast pairings

`DESIGN.md` records these reference pairings; the package test suite
recomputes every one from the generated values and asserts the recorded
ratio reproduces exactly. The live table above paints the same pairs.

| Pairing | Recorded ratio | Where it applies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy on Paper | 17.49:1 | Primary copy on the light canvas |
| Graphite on Paper | 7.81:1 | Secondary text on the light canvas |
| Deep on Paper | 7.17:1 | Readable green on light surfaces |
| Paper on Navy | 17.49:1 | Copy on the dark canvas |
| Pewter on Navy | 7.53:1 | Secondary text on the dark canvas |
| Pewter on Surface 3 | 6.00:1 | Secondary text on the raised dark step |
| Green on Navy | 11.87:1 | Green signals on dark surfaces |

Pairings introduced by the semantic role layer (White on Deep 7.80:1,
Navy on Green 11.87:1, Navy on Wash 17.15:1, Paper on Surface 2 15.19:1,
Navy on Muted 16.17:1, Graphite on Muted 7.22:1, Pewter on Surface 1
7.03:1) are recorded with their evidence in the package's
[semantic themes documentation](https://github.com/jubalm/augur-design-system/blob/main/packages/design-system/docs/semantic-themes.md).
Rules that travel with these numbers: keep every new pairing at WCAG AA
or better and recheck it before shipping; hairline borders claim no 3:1
minimum and never carry meaning alone; use the words open, closed,
pending, and final to name state, and let color only reinforce them.

## Using color well

- Usually one green signal per view; the primary action is the default
  carrier (Deep on light, Green on dark).
- On light backgrounds, raw Augur Green fails contrast for readable
  text or marks — use Deep instead. On dark backgrounds, Green carries
  the signal against Navy.
- Graphite and Pewter support the system; they do not replace primary
  copy. Wash never replaces Deep for readable green.
- Never hide meaning in color alone; state is named in words first.

The foundation proposals that sit alongside color — spacing, sizing,
radius, focus, and motion — are tracked separately in
[Foundation proposals](/foundations/proposals) and remain pending
maintainer review.
