This page is retained for historical link continuity while the documentation continues to consolidate.

The original spacing, sizing, radius, focus, motion, and state proposals have been superseded by the maintained system. Do not use this page as current design guidance.

Current destinations:

- [Brand identity](/foundations/identity) — current identity treatment and artwork boundary.
- [Fonts and typography](/foundations/fonts) — type roles and hierarchy.
- [Color system](/foundations/color) — palette, signal hierarchy, surfaces, and semantic color intent.
- [Layout and geometry](/foundations/layout-geometry) — frames, spacing, sizing, and square geometry.
- [Interaction](/foundations/interaction) — focus, hover, pressed, disabled, loading, invalid, keyboard, motion, and pointer targets.
- [Theming](/foundations/theming) — light/dark role resolution and scoping.
- [Foundation decisions](/foundations/decisions) — current decisions, superseded rules, and provenance.

Historical issue and fixture records remain useful evidence, but they should not be used to reintroduce superseded values into current implementation.
