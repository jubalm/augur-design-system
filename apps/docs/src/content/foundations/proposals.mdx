The pinned `DESIGN.md` schema cannot represent shared spacing, control
sizing, corner radius, focus treatment, motion, or interaction-state
semantics. The brand foundation defines none of these scales either.
Rather than leave every component to invent one-offs, the starter set's
smallest shared needs were written down as six proposals — FD-01 through
FD-06 — in the foundation decision record. Components may **structurally
align** with them (the Button and Card implementation does), but the
package deliberately emits no tokens for any proposed value, so nothing
can present a proposal as settled.

## FD-01 — Shared spacing scale — Proposed

Six steps on a 4px base, named after the illustrative token style in
`ARCHITECTURE.md`: 4px (`space.xs`), 8px (`space.sm`), 12px
(`space.md`), 16px (`space.lg`), 24px (`space.xl`), and 32px
(`space.2xl`), with rem equivalents at 0.25rem per 4px. The base aligns
with the adopted 16px body and 20px control line-heights, and six steps
cover every padding and gap need found in the starter component set.
While the proposal is pending, keep layout hierarchy through these
steps under review rather than introducing one-off values.

## FD-02 — Control sizing — Proposed

Control heights of 32px (`sm`), 36px (`md`), and 40px (`lg`) — matching
the shadcn size conventions the architecture adopts for familiarity —
plus 16px inline and 20px standalone icon sizes. The 36px default fits
the 20px control line-height with 8px vertical padding from the FD-01
scale and satisfies the WCAG 2.5.8 minimum target size. Open
sub-question: 36px is below the 44px mobile touch guidance; the system
currently targets a dense desktop records UI, and the touch-target
policy is an explicit maintainer decision.

## FD-03 — Corner radius — Proposed

Two values: `radius.surface` 8px for panels (Card, Dialog) and
`radius.control` 6px for controls (inputs, buttons, triggers), with the
derivation rule control = surface − 2px, mirroring the shadcn radius
convention. The tonal-depth model uses no shadows, so radius carries
grouping without competing with the fixed identity geometry of the
logo and glyph, which remains untouched. No radius tokens are emitted
while the proposal is pending.

## FD-04 — Focus treatment — Proposed spec, required behavior

A 2px `focus-visible` ring with a 2px offset, colored Deep on light
surfaces and Green on dark surfaces — one voice for intent and focus.
Pointer-triggered focus is never painted; keyboard focus is never
suppressed; focus is always a second cue alongside an existing state
change. The behavior is already **implemented structurally** in the
package theme contract (it was required by the theme work), and the
ring colors clear the 3:1 non-text minimum on every surface they paint
on — Deep on Paper 7.17:1, Green on Navy 11.87:1 — but the 2px/2px
geometry and colors remain the Proposed spec, intentionally not emitted
as tokens.

## FD-05 — Motion and reduced motion — Proposed

Two durations: 150ms for state feedback (hover, press, focus) and 250ms
for overlay enter/exit such as Dialog and collapsible regions, with
ease-out entrances and ease-in exits and no springs or decorative
loops. The brand foundation defines no motion values, so any duration
is a new system decision — none is adopted or emitted. The
**reduced-motion collapse is implemented structurally**: under
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, the package gate collapses
transition and animation durations to 0.01ms, stops looping animations,
and disables smooth scrolling, in both themes, including for
consumer-authored motion.

## FD-06 — State semantics — labels adopted, treatment Proposed

The **adopted (inherited) part**: record states are named Open, Closed,
Pending, and Final; state is carried by a text label with color as
reinforcement only; usually one green signal per view; never hide
meaning in color alone. The **Proposed part** is the interaction
contract for controls: hover as an adjacent surface step or subtle
action-color tint; pressed one step beyond hover; focus as the FD-04
ring; disabled as non-interactive with a reduced-contrast label that
remains present in the DOM and `cursor: not-allowed`; loading as a
width-preserving affordance with `aria-busy` and an unchanged action
color. Button and Card implement this treatment structurally, pending
review.

## What adoption would change

Adoption happens through maintainer review of the decision record. Once
a proposal is adopted, spacing and radius values migrate into the
`DESIGN.md` front matter via the pinned toolchain as a deliberate
migration, tokens regenerate through the standard pipeline, and the
semantic theme mapping and components re-derive from the generated
output. The open questions a reviewer needs — including the
touch-target policy and the exact spacing step names — are listed in
the [decision record](/foundations/decisions). Until then, treat every
value on this page as under review: real, written down, and not
settled.
